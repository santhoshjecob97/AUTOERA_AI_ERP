# Requirements Document

## Introduction

This specification outlines the requirements for creating a comprehensive, professional pitch deck for Autoera AI Solutions targeting automotive industry investors, partners, and enterprise customers. The pitch deck will serve as the primary presentation tool for fundraising, business development, and customer acquisition activities.

## Glossary

- **Autoera_AI_Platform**: The comprehensive B2B white-label SaaS platform offering 65+ AI models across 6 engines for automotive dealerships
- **Pitch_Deck**: A structured presentation document designed to communicate business value, market opportunity, and investment proposition
- **AI_Engine**: Specialized AI modules (Sales, Service, Finance, Insurance, Workforce, Fleet) with multiple AI models each
- **White_Label_SaaS**: Software-as-a-Service platform that allows customers to rebrand and customize the solution as their own
- **ROI_Metrics**: Return on Investment measurements demonstrating quantifiable business value (1,944% proven ROI)
- **Multi_Tenant_Architecture**: Platform design enabling complete data isolation between different customer organizations

## Requirements

### Requirement 1

**User Story:** As an investor, I want to quickly understand Autoera's market opportunity and competitive advantages, so that I can evaluate the investment potential within the first 5 minutes of the presentation.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the pitch deck is presented, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL open with a compelling problem statement that quantifies the automotive industry inefficiencies
2. WHEN presenting the solution, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL clearly articulate Autoera's unique value proposition within 2 slides
3. WHEN showing market opportunity, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL present TAM, SAM, and SOM with specific numbers for the automotive AI market
4. WHEN displaying competitive advantages, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL highlight at least 5 key differentiators that set Autoera apart from competitors
5. WHEN presenting financial projections, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL show clear path to profitability with conservative and optimistic scenarios

### Requirement 2

**User Story:** As a potential enterprise customer, I want to see concrete evidence of Autoera's capabilities and results, so that I can assess whether the platform will deliver value for my dealership.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN presenting product capabilities, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL showcase all 6 AI engines with specific use cases for each
2. WHEN showing customer success stories, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL include at least 3 detailed case studies with quantified results
3. WHEN demonstrating ROI, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL present the proven 1,944% ROI with breakdown of cost savings and revenue increases
4. WHEN displaying platform features, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL highlight White_Label_SaaS capabilities and Multi_Tenant_Architecture benefits
5. WHEN presenting implementation, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL show the 2-4 hour deployment timeline with step-by-step process

### Requirement 3

**User Story:** As a business partner, I want to understand Autoera's go-to-market strategy and partnership opportunities, so that I can evaluate potential collaboration models.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN presenting business model, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL detail all revenue streams with pricing tiers from ₹20,000 to ₹150,000 monthly
2. WHEN showing market penetration strategy, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL outline customer acquisition channels and partnership opportunities
3. WHEN displaying growth projections, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL present 3-year expansion plan with geographic and vertical growth
4. WHEN presenting team capabilities, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL highlight relevant experience and expertise in automotive and AI domains
5. WHEN showing partnership benefits, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL outline mutual value creation opportunities and revenue sharing models

### Requirement 4

**User Story:** As a presentation viewer, I want the pitch deck to be visually engaging and professionally designed, so that I can easily follow the narrative and retain key information.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN viewing any slide, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL maintain consistent branding, typography, and color scheme throughout
2. WHEN presenting data, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL use clear charts, graphs, and infographics to visualize complex information
3. WHEN transitioning between sections, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL follow a logical narrative flow from problem to solution to opportunity
4. WHEN displaying text content, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL use bullet points, short sentences, and visual hierarchy for readability
5. WHEN presenting technical concepts, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL use simple language and visual metaphors accessible to non-technical audiences

### Requirement 5

**User Story:** As a startup founder, I want the pitch deck to be adaptable for different audiences and presentation contexts, so that I can customize it for various stakeholder meetings.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN preparing for investor meetings, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL include detailed financial projections and funding requirements
2. WHEN presenting to customers, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL emphasize product benefits, ROI metrics, and implementation ease
3. WHEN adapting for different time slots, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL support both 10-minute and 30-minute presentation formats
4. WHEN customizing for specific industries, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL allow easy modification of use cases and examples
5. WHEN updating with new data, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL maintain template structure while allowing content updates

### Requirement 6

**User Story:** As a technical stakeholder, I want to understand Autoera's technology architecture and AI capabilities, so that I can evaluate the platform's technical feasibility and scalability.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN presenting technology stack, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL provide high-level architecture overview without overwhelming technical details
2. WHEN showcasing AI capabilities, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL highlight the 65+ AI models with specific examples of predictions and automation
3. WHEN discussing scalability, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL present Multi_Tenant_Architecture benefits and performance metrics
4. WHEN showing integration capabilities, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL demonstrate API-first design and third-party system connectivity
5. WHEN presenting security features, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL highlight enterprise-grade security and compliance standards

### Requirement 7

**User Story:** As a decision maker, I want clear next steps and contact information, so that I can easily follow up and initiate business discussions.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN concluding the presentation, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL provide clear call-to-action for each audience type
2. WHEN displaying contact information, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL include multiple communication channels and response time expectations
3. WHEN presenting next steps, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL outline specific actions for different engagement levels
4. WHEN showing demo options, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL provide easy scheduling links and demo preparation requirements
5. WHEN offering trial opportunities, THE Pitch_Deck SHALL present pilot program details with clear value propositions
