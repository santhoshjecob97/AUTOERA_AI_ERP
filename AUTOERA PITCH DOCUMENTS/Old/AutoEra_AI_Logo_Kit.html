Brand Usage Rules
Do's and Don'ts
✓ DO
Use the primary dark variant on navy or black backgrounds as the default choice
Use the icon mark alone at sizes below 24px where the wordmark cannot be read
Maintain minimum clear space of 1× the icon height around the full logo
Use the stacked vertical variant for square placements and social profile images
Scale the logo proportionally — never stretch or distort
Minimum logo width: 160px for horizontal, 80px for icon mark only
✗ DON'T
Don't change any colour in the logo — especially the orange brand gradient
Don't place the logo on busy photographs without a dark overlay or badge
Don't use the wordmark alone without the icon mark — they are always paired
Don't add drop shadows, outlines, or glow effects to the wordmark text
Don't recreate the logo using a different font — always use the approved SVG files
Don't use on orange, red, or warm-toned backgrounds — contrast will be lost
