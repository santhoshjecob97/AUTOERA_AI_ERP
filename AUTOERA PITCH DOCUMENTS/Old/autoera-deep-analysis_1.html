02 · The Real Problems
18 PAIN POINTS
ACROSS 6 VERTICALS

These are real, daily frustrations. Each one costs money, time, or customers.

🏪 SALES
01
📋
Lead Leakage & No Follow-Up System
Enquiries come via walk-in, phone, WhatsApp, and web — but nobody tracks them. Sales staff forget to follow up. Leads die without a conversion attempt.
CRITICAL
02
📦
Inventory Mismatch & Overstocking
Dealers are pushed OEM wholesale targets. Fast-moving variants run out. Slow variants pile up. No AI to predict what the market wants next month in Chennai.
HIGH
03
💸
Discount & Scheme Chaos
Every salesperson gives different prices. OEM schemes, exchange offers, festival discounts — nobody can track what's applied, what margin remains, what's owed.
HIGH
🔧 SERVICE
04
⏱️
Job Card Cycle is Manual & Slow
Service advisors write job cards by hand or in Excel. No digital workflow. Customer waits 20–40 mins just to drop a vehicle. Technician doesn't know job status in real time.
CRITICAL
05
🔩
Parts Stockout During Repair
Technician starts repair, discovers a part is out of stock. Vehicle sits in bay for days. Customer furious. Revenue lost. No predictive parts replenishment exists.
CRITICAL
06
👤
Customer Not Updated During Repair
Customer has no visibility into their vehicle's repair status. They call 5 times a day. Advisors waste 30% of time on status calls instead of selling or managing work.
HIGH
💰 FINANCE
07
🏦
Finance Application is Paper-Heavy
Customer submits documents physically. Bank rep visits dealership. Approvals take 3–7 days. Digital-native customers abandon the purchase. Banks like HDFC, ICICI, SBI all have different formats.
HIGH
08
📊
No EMI Structuring Intelligence
Salesperson cannot show customer a real-time EMI calculator with multiple bank options, down payment scenarios, and interest comparisons. Customer walks out to get a better deal elsewhere.
HIGH
🛡️ INSURANCE
09
📝
Insurance Renewal is Missed Revenue
Dealerships lose renewal commissions because they don't track expiry dates. No automated reminders sent 30/60/90 days before expiry. Customer renews with agent elsewhere.
HIGH
10
🚗
Claim Support is Zero
When a customer has an accident, the dealership has no system to assist with claims. No surveyor tracking, no status updates. Dealership loses a relationship opportunity and a parts repair job.
MEDIUM
👷 WORKFORCE
11
🔄
80% Sales Staff Turnover Per Year
Every time a salesperson leaves, they take customer relationships with them. No handover. CRM history is lost. The new person starts from scratch. This kills continuity and referral business.
CRITICAL
12
📈
No Performance Visibility
Managers cannot see technician efficiency, service advisor conversion, or salesperson activity in real time. They rely on end-of-month reports that are always too late to act on.
HIGH
13
🎓
No Structured Training for New Hires
New service advisors and salespeople learn by watching seniors. No digital onboarding, no product knowledge modules, no assessment. Takes 3–4 months to become productive.
MEDIUM
🚛 FLEET & ⚡ EV
14
🚌
Fleet Operators Have Zero Predictive Maintenance
Logistics companies, cab aggregators, school vans — their fleets break down mid-route. No system predicts when a vehicle will fail. Maintenance is reactive, not preventive.
CRITICAL
15
⚡
EV Dealers Don't Know Battery Health
OLA Electric, Ather, Tata EV dealers cannot predict battery degradation. Customers return complaining about range anxiety. Dealer has no data. No AI model for EV health in Indian usage patterns.
CRITICAL
16
🔌
Charging Network is Invisible
Fleet EV operators don't know where their vehicles are in charge cycles. No integration between charging station data and fleet management. Dead vehicles = missed deliveries.
HIGH
17
🗃️
Siloed Systems That Don't Talk
Dealerships use Tally for accounts, Excel for inventory, WhatsApp for lead tracking, and a basic DMS for billing. None of these systems share data. Every report is manually compiled.
CRITICAL
18
📉
No Single Source of Business Truth
Dealer principal cannot see sales pipeline + service revenue + insurance renewals + staff performance on one screen in real time. Decision-making is always delayed, always wrong.
CRITICAL

1
Lead with a Pain Question, Not a Demo
"How many enquiries did you get last month? How many did you convert? How many did you lose track of?" — let them feel the gap before you show the solution.
2
Do a Free 30-Day Audit First
Offer to audit their lead conversion, insurance renewal leakage, and service cycle time for free. Show them a report with ₹ value of money they're losing. Then pitch your platform.
3
Price as ROI, Not Per User
"You're currently losing ₹4L/month in missed renewals and leads. Our platform costs ₹25,000/month. That's a 16x ROI on the first two modules alone." — never discuss features first.
4
Start with One Dealership Group, Get a Champion
In Chennai, target mid-size dealer groups (3–10 branches). Give them a 90-day free pilot. Turn their owner into a reference. One reference in the Tamil Nadu dealer network is worth 50 cold calls.
5
Sell Modules, Not the Whole Platform
Start with ServicePulse (easiest win — shows results in 2 weeks). Then upsell InsureGuard (immediate revenue). Then SalesMind. Then expand to Fleet/EV. Land and expand.
