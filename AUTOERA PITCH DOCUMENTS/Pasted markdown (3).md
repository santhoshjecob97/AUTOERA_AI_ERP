# 5. SERVICE ADVISOR — DAILY DUTIES

A Service Advisor is one of the most important dealership employees.

Every day they need to:

1.  Call/receive customers 
2.  Understand complaints 
3.  Create job card 
4.  Inspect vehicle 
5.  Explain required work 
6.  Prepare estimate 
7.  Obtain approval 
8.  Monitor technician 
9.  Update customer 
10.  Add approved value-added services 
11.  Check completed work 
12.  Explain invoice 
13.  Deliver vehicle 
14.  Obtain feedback 

The goal isn't simply:

**"Repair the car."**

It is:

**"Repair correctly + communicate correctly + retain customer."**

# 19. MONTHLY STOCK CONTROL

This is extremely important.

### New cars

Monitor:

**Stock days**

Example:

If you have 120 cars and sell 30/month:

**Stock days ≈ 120 ÷ 30 × 30 = 120 days**

That's dangerous inventory.

Management wants the correct stock—not simply maximum stock.

# 25. EV OPERATIONS

This area deserves its own department/process.

The EV business requires different knowledge from ICE vehicles.

Staff need training around:

-  High-voltage safety 
-  Battery systems 
-  Charging 
-  Diagnostics 
-  Software 
-  EV-specific customer education 
-  Emergency procedures 
-  Battery warranty 
-  Parts 

Honda's EV after-sales approach includes dedicated service, roadside assistance and EV-specific care packages. 

And the broader Indian market is changing quickly: in August 2026, alternative-fuel passenger vehicles—including CNG, hybrid and EVs—accounted for about **42% of Indian passenger-vehicle sales**, slightly above petrol at about 41%, according to FADA data reported by Reuters. 

So an EV-ready dealership needs to treat **training, charging infrastructure, battery diagnostics and customer education** as operational priorities.

# 27. The most important management principle

Don't manage each department separately.

A customer buying a car creates **multiple revenue opportunities**:

**New Car**

→ Finance

→ Insurance

→ Accessories

→ Exchange

→ Extended Warranty

→ Service

→ Parts

→ Renewal

→ Next-car purchase

So the real objective is:

### **Customer Lifetime Value**

not simply:

### **Today's Car Sale**

Capital Honda itself describes its offering as covering the customer's broader automotive journey, including new/used cars, insurance and financing.
