## 💰 **STEP 5: TRANSACTION FEE COLLECTION**

### **How to Get Paid Transaction Fees:**

#### **Fee Collection Mechanism:**
```bash
# Method 1: Direct Bank Payment
1. Bank pays dealership monthly
2. Dealership transfers your fee
3. AUTOERA gets 1.5% of loan amount
4. Automated reconciliation

# Method 2: Customer Payment
1. Customer pays AUTOERA fee upfront
2. AUTOERA processes with bank
3. Bank pays dealership commission
4. All parties get their share

# Method 3: Hybrid Model
1. AUTOERA charges customer 1.5%
2. Bank pays dealership 1.2%
3. Dealership pays AUTOERA 0.3%
4. Total customer cost: Same as before
```

#### **Invoice & Payment Process:**
```bash
# Monthly Billing:
1. Generate invoice for bank
2. List all transactions processed
3. Calculate total transaction fees
4. Send invoice with supporting data
5. Bank pays within 30 days

# Sample Invoice:
- Total loans processed: 500
- Average loan amount: ₹8,00,000
- Total loan volume: ₹40,00,00,000
- Commission rate: 1.5%
- Total fee: ₹6,00,000
- Payment due: Within 30 days
```
