## 🏢 **Legal & Administrative Setup**

### **Phase 1: Company Incorporation (Week 1-2)**

#### **Step 1: Choose Business Structure**
```
Recommended: Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd)

Advantages:
✅ Limited liability protection
✅ Easier to raise funding
✅ Professional credibility
✅ Tax benefits and incentives
✅ Separate legal entity

Alternative: LLP (Limited Liability Partnership)
- Lower compliance costs
- Suitable for service businesses
- Harder to raise VC funding
```

#### **Step 2: Register Your Company**

**Option A: Online Registration (Recommended)**
- Platform: [MCA Portal](https://www.mca.gov.in/) or services like Razorpay Rize, Vakilsearch, LegalZoom
- Timeline: 7-10 days
- Cost: ₹10,000-₹25,000 (including professional fees)

**Documents Required:**
- [ ] PAN cards of all directors/founders
- [ ] Aadhaar cards of all directors/founders
- [ ] Passport-size photographs
- [ ] Address proof (utility bill, rental agreement)
- [ ] Digital signatures (DSC) for all directors
- [ ] Proposed company names (3-5 options)

**Registration Steps:**
1. Obtain Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) - 2-3 days
2. Apply for Director Identification Number (DIN) - 1 day
3. Reserve company name (RUN service) - 1-2 days
4. File incorporation documents (SPICe+ form) - 3-5 days
5. Receive Certificate of Incorporation - Same day after approval
6. Apply for PAN and TAN - Included in SPICe+

#### **Step 3: Post-Incorporation Compliance**

**Immediate Actions (Week 2-3):**
- [ ] Open company bank account (HDFC, ICICI, Axis recommended for startups)
- [ ] Register for GST (if turnover expected >₹20 lakhs)
- [ ] Register for Professional Tax (state-specific)
- [ ] Create company seal and letterhead
- [ ] Set up accounting software (Zoho Books, QuickBooks, Tally)

**Ongoing Compliance:**
- [ ] Annual ROC filing (within 30 days of AGM)
- [ ] Income tax returns (by September 30)
- [ ] GST returns (monthly/quarterly)
- [ ] Board meetings (minimum 4 per year)
- [ ] Maintain statutory registers

### **Phase 2: Intellectual Property Protection (Week 3-4)**

#### **Trademark Registration**

```
Steps:
1. Trademark search (₹500-₹1,000)

Cost: ₹10,000-₹25,000 (with attorney)
```

#### **Domain & Digital Assets**
- [ ] Register primary domain: autoera.ai or autoera.com
- [ ] Register country-specific domains: autoera.in, autoera.sg
- [ ] Secure social media handles: @autoera on Twitter, LinkedIn, Instagram
- [ ] Set up Google Workspace for company email (@autoera.ai)

#### **Software & Technology IP**
- [ ] Copyright source code (automatic upon creation)
- [ ] Document proprietary algorithms and AI models
- [ ] Create employee IP assignment agreements
- [ ] Consider patent filing for unique AI innovations (₹1-3 lakhs per patent)

### **Phase 3: Legal Agreements & Contracts (Week 4-6)**

#### **Founder Agreements**
```
Critical Documents:
1. Shareholders Agreement
   - Equity distribution

2. Founder Vesting Agreement

3. Non-Compete & Non-Disclosure
   - Prevent founders from competing
   - Protect confidential information
```

## 🚨 **Common Pitfalls & How to Avoid Them**

### **1. Building Without Customer Validation**
❌ **Mistake:** Building all 65 AI models before talking to customers
✅ **Solution:** Start with 1-2 customer segments, validate with 10+ customers before expanding

### **2. Premature Scaling**
❌ **Mistake:** Hiring 20 people before product-market fit
✅ **Solution:** Stay lean (5-10 people) until ₹50L+ MRR and clear demand

### **3. Ignoring Unit Economics**
❌ **Mistake:** Acquiring customers at any cost
✅ **Solution:** Track CAC and LTV from day 1, target LTV/CAC >3

### **4. Poor Cash Management**
❌ **Mistake:** Running out of cash unexpectedly
✅ **Solution:** Maintain 12-18 month runway, start fundraising at 6-9 months remaining

### **5. Founder Conflicts**
❌ **Mistake:** No clear roles, equity disputes
✅ **Solution:** Shareholders agreement with vesting, clear role definition from day 1

### **6. Overcomplicating MVP**
❌ **Mistake:** Trying to build perfect product before launch
