### **PHASE 1: FOUNDATION (Months 1-6)**

#### **1.1 Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**
```
🎯 CAC Components:
✅ Marketing Spend: ₹2 Lakh/month
✅ Sales Team Cost: ₹3 Lakh/month (2 sales reps)
✅ Tools & Software: ₹50K/month
✅ Content Creation: ₹1 Lakh/month
✅ Events & PR: ₹50K/month
✅ Total Monthly Spend: ₹7 Lakh

📈 CAC Calculation:
• Leads Generated: 500/month
• Qualified Leads: 100/month (20% conversion)
• Customers Acquired: 20/month (20% conversion)
• Monthly CAC: ₹7 Lakh / 20 customers = ₹35,000 per customer
• Annual CAC: ₹35K x 120 customers = ₹42 Lakh
```

#### **1.2 Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)**
```
💰 LTV Components:
✅ Average Monthly Revenue: ₹5,000/customer
✅ Gross Margin: 75% (₹3,750 monthly contribution)
✅ Average Lifespan: 36 months (3-year retention)
✅ Monthly LTV: ₹3,750 x 36 = ₹1.35 Lakh
✅ LTV:CAC Ratio: ₹1.35 Lakh / ₹35K = 3.85x

📊 LTV Optimization:
• Upsell Revenue: 20% additional per year
• Referral Revenue: 10% from customer referrals
• Expansion Revenue: 15% from additional products
• Adjusted LTV: ₹1.35 Lakh x 1.45 = ₹1.96 Lakh
• Optimal LTV:CAC: 5:1+ ratio
```

#### **1.3 Payback Period**
```
⏱️ Payback Calculation:
✅ Monthly Contribution Margin: ₹3,750
✅ CAC: ₹35,000
✅ Payback Period: ₹35K / ₹3,750 = 9.3 months
✅ Target Payback: <12 months for SaaS

📈 Payback Optimization:
• Reduce CAC by 20%: Payback = 7.4 months
• Increase ARPU by 25%: Payback = 7.4 months
• Combined Optimization: Payback = 5.9 months
```
