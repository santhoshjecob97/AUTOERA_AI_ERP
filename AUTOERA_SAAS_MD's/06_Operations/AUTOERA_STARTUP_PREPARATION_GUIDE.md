# ⚖️ **LEGAL PROCEDURES & COMPLIANCE**

## **7. COMPANY FORMATION**

### **Legal Structure Options**
1. **Private Limited Company**
   - Limited liability protection
   - Separate legal entity
   - Easier to raise funding
   - Complex compliance requirements

2. **Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)**
   - Limited liability for partners
   - Flexible management structure
   - Tax advantages
   - Suitable for professional services

3. **Partnership Firm**
   - Simple formation process
   - Unlimited liability
   - Not suitable for technology companies

### **Incorporation Process**
```python
class CompanyFormation:
    def __init__(self):
        self.name_reservation = "Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)"
        self.documents_preparation = "MOA + AOA"
        self.filing_application = "SPICe Form"
        self.pan_tan_application = "Company PAN and TAN"
        self.bank_account_opening = "Current Account"
        self.compliance_setup = "Statutory Registers"
```

### **Required Documents**
- **Identity Proof**: Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport
- **Address Proof**: Utility bills, bank statements
- **Business Address**: Registered office address proof
- **Memorandum of Association (MOA)**: Company objectives and structure
- **Articles of Association (AOA)**: Internal management rules
