# 🔐 **CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION**

## **1. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION**

1.1 **Confidential Information** shall mean any and all information disclosed by the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party, either directly or indirectly, in writing, orally, or by inspection of tangible objects, including but not limited to:

### **HR & Recruitment Information:**
- Candidate personal information and resumes
- Employee salary and compensation data
- Performance reviews and disciplinary records
- Employee personal and medical information
- Recruitment strategies and hiring plans
- Interview feedback and assessment results
- Employee relations matters and investigations

### **Business & Strategic Information:**
- Business plans and strategic initiatives
- Financial projections and budget information
- Customer and partner information
- Competitive analysis and market research
- Product development roadmaps
- Marketing and sales strategies

### **Technical Information:**
- Software code and technical specifications
- AI/ML algorithms and model architectures
- Database structures and data processing methods
- System architecture and integration details
- Research and development plans

### **Operational Information:**
- Internal policies and procedures
- Organizational structure and reporting relationships
- Supplier and vendor contracts
- Legal and compliance matters
- Crisis management and emergency procedures

1.2 **Confidential Information** shall also include any notes, analyses, compilations, studies, or other documents prepared by the Receiving Party that contain or reflect the Confidential Information.

1.3 **Information shall be considered Confidential Information regardless of whether it is marked as "confidential," "proprietary," "personal," or with similar legends.**

# 📋 **OBLIGATIONS OF RECEIVING PARTY**

## **2. NON-DISCLOSURE OBLIGATION**

2.1 The Receiving Party agrees:
- To hold and maintain the Confidential Information in strict confidence
- Not to disclose, publish, or disseminate Confidential Information to any third party
- Not to use Confidential Information for any purpose other than the performance of employment duties
- To protect Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care as used to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature

## **3. LIMITED USE OBLIGATION**

3.1 The Receiving Party may use Confidential Information solely for:
- Performance of assigned job responsibilities in Hiring/HR Department
- Recruitment and talent acquisition activities
- Employee relations and HR administration
- Compliance with legal and regulatory requirements
- No other commercial, personal, or third-party purposes

## **4. DATA PROTECTION OBLIGATION**

4.1 The Receiving Party shall:
- Handle all personal data in compliance with applicable privacy laws
- Implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures
- Only access information necessary for job duties
- Report any data breaches immediately to management
- Follow company data retention and deletion policies

---

# 🚫 **EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIALITY**

## **5. EXCLUSIONS**

5.1 The obligations of confidentiality shall not apply to information that:
- Is or becomes publicly known through no fault of the Receiving Party
- Is rightfully known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure
- Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to Confidential Information
- Is rightfully obtained from a third party without confidentiality restrictions
- Is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or court order (provided the Company is given reasonable notice)

---

# ⏰ **TERM AND TERMINATION**

## **6. TERM**

6.1 This Agreement shall become effective on the date first written above and shall continue for the duration of the Employee's employment with the Company plus **five (5) years** thereafter.

## **7. SURVIVAL OF OBLIGATIONS**

7.1 The obligations of confidentiality and non-use shall survive:
- Termination of employment for any reason
- Expiration of this Agreement
- Any return or destruction of Confidential Information

## **8. POST-EMPLOYMENT OBLIGATIONS**

8.1 For a period of **two (2) years** following termination of employment, the Receiving Party shall not:
- Solicit or hire Company employees
- Contact Company candidates or applicants
- Use Confidential Information to compete with the Company
- Disclose Company HR practices or strategies
