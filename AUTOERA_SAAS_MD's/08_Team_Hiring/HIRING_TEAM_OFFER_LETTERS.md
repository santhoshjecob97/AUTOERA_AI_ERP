### **ANNEXURE – I (HEAD OF TALENT)**

1. Your employment with the organization will commence from not later than [Joining Date] and you will be placed on probation for a period of 6 months (six months) from the date of your joining, your services will be confirmed only on successful completion of the said probation. The above probationary period may be extended for another 3 months at the discretion of the company.

2. During your probation, your performance will be under scrutiny / assessment by the management. If during probation, the management is not satisfied with your ability or performance, your services are liable to be terminated without notice and without assigning any reason. On completion of probation period till such time you will be intimated in writing regarding your confirmation or otherwise, you shall continue to be on probation period.

3. You will serve as **Head of Talent** of the organization and will report to **Founder & CEO**

4. You will be paid an annual CTC of **₹25,00,000/-** (Rupees Twenty Five Lakhs Only). You will work with the management of the Organization in formulating relevant KRAs/KPIs that will be applicable to you. The terms and exact details of the compensation payable and its break-up under various heads are set out in the Annexure II.

5. All forms of compensation payable to you by the Organization as set out in Annexure II shall be subject to applicable withholding and payroll taxes. We regard this information as personal and confidential and expect you to also treat it as such. Increases to these amounts are not automatic but will be based on factors such as your performance, experience and skills, as also subject to budget and financial provisions of the Organization.

6. You are expected to devote your full business time and attention to your duties with the Organization and you shall not engage in other employment, vocation or consulting activities, part-time or otherwise, without the Organization's prior written consent. Without prejudice to the foregoing, during the currency of your employment with the Organization and for a period of 2 years thereafter, you may not carry on or engage in, have any interest or act as directly or indirectly whether through partnership or as a shareholder, joint venture partner, collaborator, consultant or agent or in any other manner whatsoever, whether for profit or otherwise for business which competes with the business of the Organization; and during the subsistence of your engagement with the Organization and for a period of 2 years thereafter, you shall not attempt in any manner to solicit from any client/customer, except on behalf of the Organization, business of the type carried on by the Organization or to persuade any person, firm or entity which is a client/customer, present or future, of the Organization to cease doing business or to reduce the amount of business which any such client/customer has customarily done or might propose doing with the Organization whether or not the relationship between the Organization and such client/customer was originally established in whole or in part through your efforts.

7. Employment is offered on the basis of the information provided by you regarding your prior employment and education, as well as other relevant records. If we determine at any time that you provided false information, or that you withheld relevant information, the Organization shall be free to terminate your services at any time without prior notice or any compensation in lieu of such notice and without being liable to pay any compensation for such termination.

8. You will primarily be stationed at Organization's office(s) in [City], India in accordance with the needs and requirements of the Organization, as directed by the **Founder & CEO**

9. Your services are liable to be transferred to & at any other Unit / Offices / Branch / Group companies / Sister Concern / Subsidiary / Associate Company / Joint Venture, at any location in India / Abroad at the sole discretion of the management.

10. You will be required to travel inside and outside India in connection with the business of the Organization, as may be required by the Organization for the proper and diligent performance of your duties. The expenses of such travel shall be borne entirely by the Organization as per the Travel Policy.

11. You are not permitted to divulge the contents of this letter to any other third party. You are required to exercise full discretion towards any third party in all matters concerning the Organization, its business and policies, its management and employees.

12. It is understood that the rights to any and all data relating to the proposed business of the Organization including all Intellectual Property that may be developed by you during the course of your employment within the Organization either alone or with others pertaining to the operations or business of the Organization shall automatically belong to the Organization absolutely and you shall make no claims in respect thereto. "Intellectual Property" includes trademarks, copyrights, discoveries, inventions, licenses (software or otherwise), information, processes and similar proprietary rights, protocols, customer/client information or other written material pertaining to the business of the Organization.

13. You should not divulge or utilize, except in the furtherance of the Organization's business and with the prior written consent of the **Founder & CEO**, any information relating to the business of the Organization that comes to your knowledge in the course of your employment with the Organization or its associates. The responsibility for keeping such information secret applies not only to the period of employment but also thereafter. At times it may become difficult whether information an employee learns is confidential or publicly known. As a condition of employment, you are requested to consider work done and information received during the course of employment as confidential. Violation of the confidentiality policy of the Organization will call for strict disciplinary action.

14. You will be entitled to leaves, amenities, reimbursement facilities and such other entitlements as may be applicable in accordance with the Organization's policy in force from time to time.

15. You shall abide by the "Code of Ethics", the employment policies of the Organization as may be set out by the Organization in its employee handbook/manual, and any other Organization policies, procedures, rules or guidelines in connection with the day-to-day operations of the Organization, the behavior of its employees, the overall management of the Organization and its clients/customers, as amended from time to time by the Organization. These will be deemed an integral part of the terms of your employment. The Organization shall have the right to change, alter, supplement or rescind its employment procedures, benefits or policies, including its incentive or bonus policies and plans, at any time in its sole and absolute discretion without notice. You are responsible for reviewing and complying with the same.

16. You will be governed by the laid down "Code of Ethics" of the company and if there is any breach of the same or nonconformance of contractual obligation or with the terms and conditions laid down in this agreement, your service can be terminated without any notice, notwithstanding, any other terms and conditions stipulated, herein the company reserves the right to invoke other legal remedies as it deems fit to protect its legitimate interest.

17. If at any time in our opinion, which is final in this matter you are found guilty of fraud, dishonest, disobedience, disorderly behavior, negligence, indiscipline, habitual absence, continuous non performer), absence from duty without permission or any other conduct considered by us deterrent to our interest or of violation of one or more terms of this letter, your services may be terminated without notice and on account of reason of any of the acts or omission the company shall be entitled to recover the damages from you.

18. The Company shall review the performance and salary of the Employee annually, in accordance with the policy of the Company at that time. Increases in salary are not automatically granted at every review as the same is related to the level of performance which would be decided by the management after proper assessment of the performance of the employee.

19. The correspondence between the organization and you will be in-person or at the address given below: [Candidate Address]. Any communication (Registered A.D/ Speed Post/Though courier) sent at the above address will be deemed as proper service. In case there will be any change in your address, you will intimate the HR Department in writing within 3 days from the date of such change otherwise the existing given address will be deemed as proper service.

20. Your continuous absence for eight days (8 days) or more without any communication to the Organization invariably proves your voluntary abandonment of services and accordingly your name will be removed from the muster roll.

21. In the event of your resignation, you are required to give **Three Months'** notice to the organization. If you are unable to give the organization such required notice, the company shall recover a proportionate salary in lieu of the shortage of notice period thereof.

22. On resignation or termination of service, your full and final accounts will be settled on furnishing a certificate from the Head of Accounts Department, that you do not owe any loan or amount to the management and you have handed over possession of the management's property entrusted to you.

23. You will be bound by any rules and regulations enforced by the management from time to time in relation to conduct, discipline, medical leave and holidays or on any matters relating to service conditions which will be deemed as rules, regulations and order in the part of these terms of employment.

24. You will be Retired on attaining the age of 58 years. Date of birth entered in your service record will be considered for the purpose of determining your date of retirement. Date of birth on the basis of which you are appointed will not be permitted to be altered under any circumstances.

25. Your continuance in service, is subject to you remaining physically and mentally fit. As and when required by the management, you will submit yourself to medical examination, by a physician appointed by the management for the purpose.

Please signify your acceptance of the terms and conditions stipulated above by signing and returning the duplicate copy of this letter. We welcome you to join us for an exuberant and rewarding career.

**Yours truly,**  
**For AUTOERA TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED**
