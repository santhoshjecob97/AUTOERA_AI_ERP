# ⚖️ **LEGAL & CONTRACTUAL FRAMEWORK**

## **EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT COMPONENTS**

```python
class EmploymentContract:
    def __init__(self):
        self.basic_information = "Employee and employer details"
        self.position_details = "Job title, department, reporting"
        self.compensation = "Salary, benefits, equity"
        self.work_arrangements = "Work hours, location, remote work"
        self.confidentiality = "NDA and confidentiality obligations"
        self.intellectual_property = "IP assignment and ownership"
        self.termination = "Notice period and termination clauses"
        self.governing_law = "Applicable law and jurisdiction"
```

## **KEY CONTRACT CLAUSES**

1. **Employment Period**: At-will employment with mutual notice period
2. **Compensation and Benefits**: Detailed salary, bonus, equity structure
3. **Work Arrangements**: Flexible hours, remote work policies
4. **Confidentiality**: NDA obligations and information protection
5. **Intellectual Property**: Assignment of inventions and IP rights
6. **Non-Compete**: Reasonable post-employment restrictions
7. **Termination**: 3-month notice period for director level
8. **Dispute Resolution**: Arbitration and governing law

## **EQUITY AGREEMENT**

### **ESOP Terms:**
- **Grant Amount**: 5% of fully diluted equity
- **Exercise Price**: Fair market value at grant date
- **Vesting Schedule**: 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff
- **Expiration**: 10 years from grant date
- **Termination Provisions**: Accelerated vesting on change of control
