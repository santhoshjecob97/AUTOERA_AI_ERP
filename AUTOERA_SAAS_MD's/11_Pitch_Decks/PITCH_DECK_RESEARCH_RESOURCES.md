## 🎯 RESEARCH METHODOLOGY

### Step-by-Step Research Process
1. **Market Sizing (Top-Down)**
   - Start with global automotive market size
   - Narrow to software/SaaS segment
   - Calculate TAM, SAM, SOM

2. **Competitive Analysis**
   - Identify direct and indirect competitors
   - Analyze pricing, features, funding
   - Position your unique value proposition

3. **Customer Validation**
   - Survey potential customers
   - Validate pain points and willingness to pay
   - Gather testimonials and case studies

4. **Financial Benchmarking**
   - Research SaaS metrics for your stage
   - Compare to similar companies
   - Validate unit economics assumptions

5. **Trend Analysis**
   - Identify market drivers and trends
   - Validate technology adoption
   - Assess regulatory impact

## 💡 PRO TIPS FOR RESEARCH

1. **Use Multiple Sources**: Never rely on single source for key statistics
2. **Recent Data**: Prioritize data from 2022-2024 for accuracy
3. **Geographic Relevance**: Separate global and India-specific data
4. **Methodology Consistency**: Ensure market sizing uses consistent definitions
5. **Primary Research**: Supplement with customer interviews and surveys
6. **Competitive Intelligence**: Use tools like SimilarWeb, SEMrush for competitor analysis
7. **Financial Validation**: Cross-check financial projections with industry benchmarks
8. **Regulatory Updates**: Stay current with policy changes affecting automotive sector
