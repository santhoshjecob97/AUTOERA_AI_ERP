# **PHASE 1: HOW TO FIND CLIENTS (LEAD GENERATION)**

Your goal is to build a list of **100 Potential Clients** in Chennai/Tamil Nadu to start with.
Focus on **Automotive Dealerships, Service Centers, Manufacturing Units** first.

### **1. Google Maps (The Best Free Source)**
*   **Action:** Search for specific businesses in your target area.
    *   *"Car service centers in Guindy, Chennai"*
    *   *"Textile manufacturers in Tirupur"*
    *   *"Auto component manufacturers in Ambattur"*
*   **What to Extract:** Business Name, Phone Number, Website.
*   **Tip:** Look for businesses with **good ratings (4.0+)** but **bad websites** (or no website). They value reputation but need tech help.

### **2. JustDial & IndiaMART**
*   **Action:** Search for B2B categories.
    *   *Category: "Automotive Repair Equipment Dealers", "Industrial Machinery Manufacturers"*
*   **What to do:** Don't just email. **Call the mobile numbers** listed. These are often direct lines to owners.

### **3. LinkedIn (For Mid-Sized Companies)**
*   **Action:** Search for titles: "Owner", "Managing Director", "Operations Manager".
*   **Filter:** Location = "Chennai" / "Tamil Nadu", Industry = "Automotive" / "Manufacturing".
*   **Tip:** Connect with a note: *"Hi [Name], I'm a local founder building AI tech for [Industry]. innovative solution for [Industry] businesses in TN. Would love to connect."*

### **4. Industry Associations**
*   **Automotive:** FADA (Federation of Automobile Dealers Associations) directory.
*   **Manufacturing:** Local MSME clusters (e.g., Ambattur Industrial Estate Association).
*   **Action:** Visit their websites for member directories.

# **PHASE 2: HOW TO CONTACT CLIENTS (OUTREACH)**

Don't spam. Be personal. You are a **StartupTN Partner**, use that credibility.

## 📞 **A. The Cold Call Script (Phone)**
*Goal: Get a meeting/demo. Do not sell the product on the call.*

**You:** "Hi, is this [Name]? I'm Santhosh from **Autoera AI** in Chennai."
**Client:** "Yes, who is this?"
**You:** "I'm calling because we’ve built an AI tool specifically for **[their industry, e.g., Car Service Centers]** that helps reduce operational costs by **30%**. We are also a registered **StartupTN vendor** offering subsidized rates."
**You:** "I’m not trying to sell you anything right now. I just want to show you a **10-minute demo** of how it works. Would you be open to seeing how it could help [Business Name]?"
**If Yes:** "Great, I can come by tomorrow at 11 AM or do a Zoom call. Which works for you?"

# **PHASE 3: HOW TO SHOW THE PROTOTYPE (THE DEMO)**

When you get the meeting, successful demos follow this structure:

### **1. The "Hook" (2 Minutes)**
*   Don't start with login screens. Start with the **Problem**.
*   **Say:** "Most [Industry] businesses struggle with [Problem, e.g., tracking vehicle status]. Our tool solves exactly that."

### **2. The "Solution" (5 Minutes)**
*   Show the **Dashboard**: "This is your control center. You can see everything happening in your business here."
*   Show **ONE Killer Feature**: Pick the most impressive AI feature (e.g., Predictive Maintenance or Sales Forecasting).
*   *Make it real:* "Imagine being able to know which machine will fail before it happens."

### **3. The "Offer" (3 Minutes)**
*   **Say:** "Usually, enterprise software like this costs ₹50,000/month."
*   **Reveal:** "But with Autoera, and the StartupTN subsidy, you get this for just **₹14,000/month**."
*   **Close:** "We can set up a **15-day free trial** for you today. Shall we proceed?"
