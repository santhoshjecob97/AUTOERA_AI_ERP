### **1. COMPANY SETUP & LEGAL STRUCTURE**

#### **1.1 Company Registration (India)**
```
📋 Entity Types:
✅ Private Limited Company (Recommended)
• Minimum 2 Directors, 2 Shareholders
• ₹1 Lakh minimum capital
• 15-20 days registration
• Best for investor funding

✅ LLP (Limited Liability Partnership)
• Minimum 2 Partners
• No minimum capital
• 10-15 days registration
• Good for service-based

✅ OPC (One Person Company)
• Single founder
• ₹50K minimum capital
• 10-15 days registration
• Limited to ₹50 Lakh turnover

📊 Registration Process:
1. Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)
2. Director Identification Number (DIN)
3. Name Approval (RUN Form)
4. MOA/AOA Preparation
5. Incorporation Filing (SPICe+)
6. PAN/TAN Application
7. Bank Account Opening
8. GST Registration
```

✅ Professional Tax Registration
