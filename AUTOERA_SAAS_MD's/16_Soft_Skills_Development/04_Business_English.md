## **✍️ WRITTEN BUSINESS ENGLISH**

### **1. Email Excellence**
```bash
Professional Email Structure:
Subject Line:
• Specific and action-oriented
• 6-8 words maximum
• Keywords for easy searching
• Urgency indicators when needed

Greeting:
• Formal: "Dear Mr./Ms./Dr. [Last Name]"
• Semi-formal: "Hello [First Name]"
• Team: "Dear Team/Colleagues"
• Avoid: "Hey" or "Hi there"

Body Structure:
• Opening: Purpose statement
• Middle: Key details and information
• Closing: Call to action or next steps
• Sign-off: Professional closing

AUTOERA Email Standards:
✅ Clear Subjects: "Q4 Revenue Projections - AUTOERA AI"
✅ Professional Greetings: "Dear [Name]" or "Hello [Name]"
✅ Structured Content: Purpose → Details → Action
✅ Professional Signatures: Full name, title, contact info
```

## **🗣️ SPOKEN BUSINESS ENGLISH**

### **1. Meeting Communication**
```bash
Meeting Language:
Opening a Meeting:
• "Good [morning/afternoon], everyone. Thank you for joining."
• "Let's begin with a quick round of introductions."
• "Our agenda for today includes..."
• "We have [X] minutes allocated for this discussion."

During Discussion:
• "That's an interesting perspective. Could you elaborate?"
• "I see your point, but I'd like to add..."
• "Building on what [Name] said..."
• "Let me clarify that point..."

Seeking Input:
• "What are your thoughts on this approach?"
• "Does anyone have experience with this?"
• "How do you see this impacting our timeline?"
• "Are there any concerns about this direction?"

AUTOERA Meeting Style:
✅ Inclusive: Encourage all participants to contribute
✅ Focused: Keep discussions on track
✅ Respectful: Acknowledge all viewpoints
✅ Action-Oriented: End with clear next steps
```

### **2. Presentation Language**
```bash
Presentation Phrases:
Introduction:
• "Today, I'd like to share with you..."
• "The purpose of this presentation is..."
• "By the end of this session, you will..."
• "Let me start by giving you some context..."

Main Content:
• "Let's examine this data..."
• "This chart illustrates..."
• "The key takeaway here is..."
• "Moving on to our next point..."

Transitions:
• "Now that we've covered X, let's discuss Y..."
• "Building on that foundation..."
• "This leads us to our next topic..."
• "To summarize this section..."

Conclusion:
• "In conclusion, the main points are..."
• "To summarize our discussion..."
• "The recommended action is..."
• "Thank you for your attention. Are there any questions?"

AUTOERA Presentation Style:
✅ Engaging: Interactive and dynamic delivery
✅ Clear: Simple language and explanations
✅ Professional: Confident and authoritative tone
✅ Audience-Focused: Address audience needs and questions
```

### **3. Negotiation Language**
```bash
Negotiation Phrases:
Opening Positions:

• "Based on the scope you've described..."
• "We typically offer this service for..."
• "Considering the requirements, our proposal is..."

Seeking Information:
• "Could you tell me more about your priorities?"
• "What timeline are you working with?"
• "How does this fit into your budget?"
• "What other options are you considering?"

Making Concessions:
• "We could adjust the timeline to..."
• "We're willing to include additional..."
• "We can offer a discount if..."
• "Let's explore a different approach..."

Closing Agreements:
• "Based on our discussion, I propose..."
• "Are you comfortable with these terms?"
• "Let's document these agreements..."

AUTOERA Negotiation Approach:
✅ Collaborative: Win-win solution focus
```
