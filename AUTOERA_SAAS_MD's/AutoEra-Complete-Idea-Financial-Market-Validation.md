## 3. Pain Point Mapping & Customer Validation

### Service Centers
- Manual op (70%), poor workflow, inventory lock, low technician utilization.
- AI modules directly address diagnosis, workflow, inventory, customer conversion.
- **Validated demand:** 12 pilot requests, 85% interview willingness to pay.
- **Pricing (India):** ₹9,999 – ₹49,999/month.

### Insurance Companies
- Fraud loss rate: ₹15,000 Cr/year; slow manual assessment.
- AI damage detection, predictive workflows cut fraud 8%, claim time >80%.
- **Price willingness:** ₹1–2 Cr/year (large insurers).

### Dealerships & Fleet
- Inefficient lead management, high ongoing maintenance, fuel waste.
- Gain: workflow AI, fleet module, telematics integration.
- Market gap for integrated SaaS proven across interviews.

## 6. Risks & Recommendations

### Key Risks
- **Technology:** AI models may fall short; validate accuracy with client data.
- **Sales Cycles:** B2B sales are long; focus on mid-market expansion.
- **Competition:** Incumbent vendors may start to build similar features; defend with IP filings and fast execution.
- **Team:** Hiring CTO/AI engineers is urgent for IP and platform scaling.

### Mitigation
- **Launch narrow MVP:** Service AI + Predictive Maintenance to validate market before full platform.
- **File core patents** early during pilot phase.
- **Target mid-sized dealerships** for easier early wins and feedback loops.
- **Focus on execution:** Hire core tech team before scaling product breadth.
