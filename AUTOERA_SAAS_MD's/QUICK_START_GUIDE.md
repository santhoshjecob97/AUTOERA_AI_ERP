## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### Plans Page Not Showing
1. Hard refresh: Ctrl+Shift+R
2. Check sidebar for "Plans & Pricing"
3. Clear browser cache

### Voice AI Button Missing
1. Go to Service Engine
2. Look in "AI Actions" column
3. Blue phone icon next to "Predictive Diag"

### Login Issues
1. Use any email format
2. Password can be anything (demo)
3. Role auto-assigned based on email
