## 🔍 Quick Access Guide

### To Use Individual Voice Calls:
1. Navigate to any engine
2. Find the data table (Leads, Jobs, Loans, Policies, Vehicles, Employees)
3. Look for the **phone icon** in the Actions column
4. Click to initiate individual call

### To Use Bulk Campaigns:
1. Navigate to any engine
2. Scroll down to find the **Voice AI section** (purple/indigo gradient)
3. Click **"Launch Campaign"** to expand
4. Choose **"Bulk Upload CSV"** or **"Connect Google Sheets"**
5. Upload your data
6. Click **Play** to start campaign
