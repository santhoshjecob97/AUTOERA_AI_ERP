## 2. Net Dealership Monthly ROI Calculation

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Direct Monthly Labor Savings} &= \text{₹}1,40,000.00 \\
\text{Gross Profit from Incremental ROs} &= \text{₹}6,18,375.00 \\
\hline
\mathbf{Total\ Monthly\ Net\ Value\ Created} &= \mathbf{\text{₹}7,58,375.00} \\
\text{AutoEra Enterprise SaaS Fee} &= \text{₹}45,000.00 \\
\hline
\mathbf{Net\ Dealership\ Monthly\ ROI} &= \frac{\text{₹}7,58,375.00 - \text{₹}45,000.00}{\text{₹}45,000.00} = \mathbf{15.85\times\ ROI\ (1,585\%)}
\end{aligned}$$
