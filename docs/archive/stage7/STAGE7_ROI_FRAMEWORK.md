## 1. Commercial Value Realization Framework

| Dealership Operational Metric | Baseline Dealership (Manual BDC) | AutoEra AI Pilot Target | Measurement Method | Commercial Impact |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Inbound Call Answer Rate** | $72\%$ ($28\%$ missed after hours) | $\mathbf{99.5\%}$ (24/7 AI Reception) | Twilio Call Records | Zero lost service leads |
| **Average Speed to Answer** | $45\text{ seconds}$ | $\mathbf{< 2\text{ seconds}}$ | Telephony Call Log | Improved CSAT / CSI |
| **Service Appointment Conversion** | $48\%$ | $\mathbf{72\%}$ ($+24\%$ target) | `sales_appointment` DB | Higher workshop intake |

## 2. Dealership Monthly ROI Calculation (Medium 3S Dealership)

$$\text{Monthly Operational Cost Reduction} = \text{₹}2,55,000$$
$$\text{Incremental Service Revenue from 24/7 Booking} \approx \text{₹}4,20,000$$
$$\text{Total Monthly Commercial Value Created} = \mathbf{\text{₹}6,75,000}$$
$$\text{AutoEra Enterprise SaaS Fee} = \text{₹}45,000 / \text{month}$$
$$\text{Net Dealership Monthly ROI} = \frac{\text{₹}6,75,000 - \text{₹}45,000}{\text{₹}45,000} = \mathbf{14.0\times\ ROI\ (1,400\%)}$$
