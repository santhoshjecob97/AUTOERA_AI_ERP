# Autoera AI Solutions - Brand Identity Guidelines

## Brand Overview

Autoera AI Solutions represents innovation, reliability, and transformation in the automotive industry. Our brand communicates cutting-edge AI technology made accessible and practical for automotive dealerships.

## Brand Values

- **Innovation**: Leading-edge AI technology that transforms operations
- **Reliability**: Enterprise-grade platform with proven 1,944% ROI
- **Accessibility**: Complex AI made simple and affordable
- **Partnership**: White-label approach that empowers our customers' brands
- **Results**: Data-driven solutions that deliver measurable business impact

## Visual Identity

### Logo Usage

**Primary Logo**: Autoera AI Solutions with automotive-inspired iconography
- Use on light backgrounds with sufficient contrast
- Maintain minimum clear space of 2x logo height
- Never distort, rotate, or modify logo proportions

**Secondary Logo**: Simplified "Autoera AI" for smaller applications
- Use when space is limited
- Maintain legibility at all sizes

### Typography

#### Primary Font: Inter
- **Headings**: Inter Bold (700)
- **Subheadings**: Inter SemiBold (600)
- **Body Text**: Inter Regular (400)
- **Captions**: Inter Medium (500)

#### Secondary Font: Roboto Mono
- **Code/Technical**: Roboto Mono Regular (400)
- **Data/Numbers**: Roboto Mono Medium (500)

### Font Hierarchy

```
H1 - Inter Bold, 48px, Autoera Blue
H2 - Inter SemiBold, 36px, Professional Gray
```

## Iconography

### Style Guidelines
- **Line Style**: 2px stroke weight, rounded caps
- **Fill Style**: Solid fills for primary icons
- **Size**: 24px, 32px, 48px standard sizes
- **Color**: Autoera Blue primary, Innovation Orange for highlights

### Icon Categories
- **Automotive**: Cars, dealerships, service, parts
- **AI/Technology**: Neural networks, algorithms, automation
- **Business**: Growth, revenue, partnerships, success
- **Data**: Charts, analytics, predictions, insights

## Layout Principles

### Grid System
- **12-column grid** for desktop layouts
- **8-column grid** for tablet layouts  
- **4-column grid** for mobile layouts
- **24px base unit** for spacing and alignment

### White Space
- **Generous margins**: Minimum 48px around content areas
- **Consistent spacing**: Use multiples of 24px (24, 48, 72, 96)
- **Breathing room**: Allow content to breathe, avoid cramped layouts

### Visual Hierarchy
1. **Primary Focus**: Largest, boldest, Autoera Blue
2. **Secondary Focus**: Medium size, SemiBold, Professional Gray
3. **Supporting Info**: Regular weight, smaller size
4. **Metadata**: Smallest, Light Gray, minimal prominence

## Presentation Guidelines

### Slide Composition
- **Title Area**: Top 20% of slide, Autoera Blue background
- **Content Area**: Middle 60%, white background with structured content
- **Footer Area**: Bottom 20%, company info and slide numbers

### Content Density
- **Maximum 7 bullet points** per slide
- **Maximum 50 words** per bullet point
- **One key message** per slide
- **Supporting visuals** for complex concepts

### Data Visualization
- **Consistent colors**: Use brand palette for all charts
- **Clear labels**: All axes and data points labeled
- **Meaningful titles**: Descriptive chart titles that tell the story
- **Source attribution**: Always cite data sources

## Usage Examples

### Correct Usage ✅
- Logo on clean, contrasting backgrounds
- Consistent color application across all materials
- Proper font hierarchy and spacing
- Professional, clean layouts with ample white space

### Incorrect Usage ❌
- Logo on busy or low-contrast backgrounds
- Mixing fonts outside the approved typography system
- Inconsistent color usage or off-brand colors
- Cramped layouts with insufficient white space
- Distorted or modified logo proportions
