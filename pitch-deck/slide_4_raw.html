🌐 Global Market Drivers & Indian Nucleus

India

World's 3rd Largest Auto Market: 30,000+ FADA dealer outlets employing 5M+ people. Dealerships urgently transitioning from legacy on-prem DMS to cloud AI ERP.

TN Base

Tamil Nadu Auto Capital ("Detroit of Asia"): 70% of India's electric 2W, 40% of electric 4W, 1,200+ major dealerships within 300km corridor for hyper-rapid pilot iteration.

Global

High-Margin Emerging Corridors: Southeast Asia (Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand) and GCC/Middle East have identical right-hand/independent dealer structures with 3x higher SaaS ARPU ($399–$899/mo).

Tailwind

GST E-Invoicing + EV Boom: Strict regulatory compliance mandates and complex EV battery service workflows make old software obsolete.
