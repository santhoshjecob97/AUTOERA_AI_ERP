Why existing DMS vendors can't just "add AI"

AI-native means the data model supports embeddings , the API supports tool calling , and the workflow supports ActionProposals . This is architectural — you can't bolt it onto a 15-year-old monolith. AutoEra was built AI-native from Day 1.
